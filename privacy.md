# Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

## Introduction

StoryLens is an AI-powered application that transforms fairy tales and children's stories into visual storyboards.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use StoryLens.

## Information We Collect

StoryLens may collect:

* Story titles and prompts submitted by users
* Generated story content and storyboard data
* Technical information required to operate the service, such as device and browser information
* Usage and diagnostic information

## How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

* Generate stories and visual storyboards
* Improve the quality and reliability of the service
* Monitor performance and troubleshoot issues
* Protect the service from misuse

## Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

We may use third-party service providers, including AI and cloud infrastructure providers, to process requests and operate the service.

## Data Retention

Submitted content may be stored for a limited period to improve service reliability, troubleshoot issues, and enhance user experience.

## Children's Privacy

StoryLens is intended to create content for children; however, it is designed to be used under the supervision of a parent, guardian, educator, or other responsible adult. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
