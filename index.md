# StoryLens

Every fairy tale becomes a picture story.

Transform fairy tales and children's stories into illustrated scene-by-scene storyboards. Just enter the title of a fairy tale or children's story, and StoryLens uses AI to generate the story, extract characters, and visualize key moments to bring the adventure to life.

## How it works

1. Enter a story title.
2. StoryLens generates the narrative.
3. Characters are identified and described.
4. Scenes are transformed into a visual storyboard.

Perfect for young readers, parents, and educators.
